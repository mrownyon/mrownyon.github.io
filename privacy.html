Privacy policy

MrOwny n8n

Effective 19 November 2026

Who we are

MrOwny is a video channel on YouTube and TikTok. MrOwny n8n is the private tool its owner uses to post the channel's own videos. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used by no one else.

What the tool accesses

  • YouTube: with the permission the owner gives through Google sign-in, it uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and sets their title, description, tags and publication time.
  • TikTok: with the permission the owner gives through TikTok sign-in, it sends the owner's own videos to the owner's TikTok inbox as drafts, which the owner then finishes and posts in the TikTok app.

It does not read, collect or keep any information about anyone else: not the viewers, the subscribers or the people who comment.

What it keeps

Only the access and refresh tokens that Google and TikTok issue when the owner signs in, kept encrypted on the owner's computer so the tool can keep posting. There is no other server or database. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone or used for advertising.

Google user data

MrOwny n8n's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool uses YouTube API Services: see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

This website

These pages are plain static pages: no cookies, no analytics, no forms, and nothing loaded from other sites. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record visits under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw the tool's access at any time: for Google, on myaccount.google.com/permissions; for TikTok, in the TikTok app's settings, under the apps connected to the account. The tokens kept on the owner's computer then stop working.

Contact and changes

Questions: through the channel's YouTube page. If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
